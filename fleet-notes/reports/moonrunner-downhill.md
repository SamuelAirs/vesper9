# moonrunner-downhill (cloud session, branch claude/project-thread-9byw8y)

1. **Outcome.** Moonrunner (`web/apps/runner.js`) is rebuilt as an Alto's-style one-button downhill run with the depth pattern of Perihelion and Outpost: zones, survey orders and levels with unlocks, a workshop, a daily run, zen riding and a depot. Everything was checked in Node and headless Chromium only; nothing was verified on the device.
2. **Details.**
   - Play: tap to jump; hold more than 0.12 s in the air to turn backwards; release so the sled lands flat. Flips, grinds, rilles and boulders are tricks that build a combo (x1 to x5; surge from 3). The first 250 m forgive crashes; hover pads (workshop) catch crashes later.
   - Lamps: in the air, a green, amber or red fill for a clean, stumbling or crashing landing, brighter as the ground nears, plus a white spot that runs across once per turn while turning. On the ground: left = speed, middle = combo clock, right = the next hazard (amber boulder, red rille, cyan vent, violet cable), blinking at jump time. Grinding is a violet chase. Dark on title and result; dim spot in the depot.
   - Smoothness: the terrain is a Catmull-Rom curve, the backgrounds are functions of world x, and `draw()` extrapolates the camera by the time since the last update.
   - Save schema 2 (`migrateSave`) keeps the first release's runs, last result and milestone, and turns each old 100 m milestone into 5 shards.
   - Catalog entry: new description, controls, `record` names. README and OPERATOR.md updated.
3. **Verification.** `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`: 845 pass, 1 fail. The failure is Perihelion's "planning bot crosses every region" (seed 3003 falls) and it fails the same way on `main` c741cca in this environment. Python 189 OK (1 skipped). `build-catalog.py --check` clean. browser-smoke, extension-smoke, host-browser and voice-host (37/37) all pass. Screenshots of every zone, the depot, the workshop and the result were taken with `scripts/dev-shot.cjs` and looked at.
4. **Not done / uncertain.** Feel on the real display and the lamps are unverified. The flip timing (7.6 rad/s) and landing tolerance may want tuning after Sam plays. Console best scores are now points (metres plus tricks), so the old metres best stays on the scoreboard until it is beaten. Host-level draw interpolation would help every game (not done; `web/main.js` is shared).
