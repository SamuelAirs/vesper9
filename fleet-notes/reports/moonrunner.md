# moonrunner (cloud session, branch claude/project-thread-9byw8y)

1. **Outcome.** Moonrunner (`web/apps/runner.js`) is reworked from the Alto's-style downhill build into a Tiny Wings-style hill-flyer, after Sam played the first build ("the flipping is too fast and linear, and there's no sense of speed or obstacles"). The depth from the first build stays: zones, survey orders and levels with unlocks, a workshop, a daily run, zen riding and a depot. Everything was checked in Node and headless Chromium only; nothing was verified on the device.
2. **Details.**
   - Play: hold to dive (heavy: gains speed down a slope, loses it on a climb), let go to fly off the crest. A landing within about 24 degrees of a downslope is a perfect slide (speed burst, +0.5 s daylight, chain points); three in a row is fever (double points, higher top speed); far off the slope is a thud that breaks the chain. Flips are gone.
   - The run is timed by daylight: 40 s, +25 s per new zone; at night the sled coasts to a stop. No crashes.
   - Obstacles: dust pits (II), rilles past the crests that need about 76 km/h at the rim (III; falling in costs 8 s), boost crystals (IV), gas vents (V), all on the far side (VI).
   - Speed: the camera pulls back with speed and height, streaks cross the sky above 450 px/s, the HUD shows km/h, and the terminator darkens the screen from the left as the daylight runs out.
   - Lamps: left = speed (cyan while diving); middle = in the air green when a landing now would be perfect, amber when not, on the ground the chain; right = daylight (cyan, amber under 15 s, blinking red under 8 s), blinking red for a rille ahead of a sled too slow to clear it. Fever is a white chase. Dark on title and result.
   - Save schema 3 (`migrateSave`) migrates schema 1 (first release) and schema 2 (the downhill build: level, shards, sleds, records kept; the level's orders restart; hover pads and headlamp refunded as shards).
   - Catalog: new description, controls (HOLD TO DIVE · LET GO TO FLY) and `record` names. README, OPERATOR.md, ENGINE.md, WORKLOG.md updated.
