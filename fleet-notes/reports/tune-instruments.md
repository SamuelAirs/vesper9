## 2. Details
**Atmosphere** (`web/apps/utilities.js`; pure helpers appended to `web/engine/math.js`)
- Tiles: temperature and humidity with a one-hour trend (arrow and rate; steady below 0.3 C/h or 1.5 %/h; "collecting" with fewer than 3 samples or under 20 minutes of span) and the comfort verdict. Strip: dew point, feels like, absolute humidity, today's low and high with times (since local midnight, from the 5-minute averages plus the live reading). Temperatures and rates follow `tempUnit`; absolute humidity is always g/m3 (a density).
- Formulas: dew point = Magnus, Alduchov and Eskridge (1996) constants a=17.625, b=243.04 (-45..60 C, RH>0, else null). Feels like = NWS heat index (Steadman simple formula, then Rothfusz regression with the NWS low/high humidity adjustments), defined only for 80..120 F and RH 40..100, otherwise the plain air temperature labelled AIR TEMP. Absolute humidity = Bolton (1980) saturation vapour pressure plus ideal gas (216.74 e / T), -30..60 C. Comfort: DRY <30 %, COMFORTABLE 30-60, HUMID 60-70, VERY HUMID >70 (EPA 30-50 %, ASHRAE 55 / mould guidance cap 60 %).
- Lamps: all three one steady dim colour (25 % of full before the global lamp level): amber dry, green comfortable, cyan humid, violet very humid. Dark if the reading is older than 2 minutes or the node is disconnected, and dark in `dispose()`. In a timer's last ten seconds it shows the host's amber 3-2-1 countdown (holding the lamps would otherwise suppress the host's).
- History: Range action cycles 24 HOURS / 7 DAYS / 30 DAYS; two charts side by side (SVG, 12 px axis labels, axes in the chosen unit, dates on the x axis beyond 36 h). History reloads once a minute. Markup is only sent when it changed: the old "N s AGO" text is bucketed to change at most once a minute (new export `sensorFreshness`; `sensorStatus` kept). Actions `range`, `refresh`, `home`.
- The whole screen including both action rows fits 1024x600.

**Node Scope:** rows LINK, FIRMWARE, SENSOR BUS (`0x44 - 1204 OK - 2 FAIL`), SENSOR LAST ERROR (esp_err names), LAMP LEVEL. `main.js` does not keep `link`, `fw` or `sensor` from `node_status` in `state.device`, so the page keeps its own copy from the `node_status` event (about once a second); until one arrives it says AWAITING STATUS (SIMULATOR on the simulator, which sends none). Reset on `node_reset` and disconnect. Lamp level low shows a notice; level off labels the test action "... / LAMPS OFF", shows a notice and a toast (the request is still made; the host scales it to dark). At the default level the labels are unchanged (`browser-smoke` clicks "TEST COLOUR / OFF"). Every action has a stable id; mic-level events re-render at most twice a second (audit item 3, proposal C), a 1 s tick catches the trailing value. Also fixed: the footnote listed the superseded pin map; it now follows `docs/HARDWARE.md`.

**Chronometer:** large readout (52 px) and progress bar for the nearest running timer, at most three other timers below with "+N MORE". Lamps: a bar draining from the right across the three lamps, green to amber, about 30 %; for remaining <= 10 s the host's amber 3/2/1 pattern; dark when finished so the service's triple blink is not fought (the host suspends flushing for its 1200 ms). Lamps are only taken once a timer runs and are zeroed in `dispose()`. Ticks no longer rebuild unchanged markup.

**Field Notes:** `mic` events no longer refetch sessions and transcript unless mode or session changed; a final speech line renders once (the reload) instead of twice. **Calibration:** stable ids added to every action lacking one; lamp settings untouched.

Catalog descriptions of the three apps updated, `web/apps/catalog.js` regenerated. `docs/OPERATOR.md` sections Chronometer, Atmosphere, Node Scope rewritten. CSS appended at the end of `web/style.css` only (atmo-*, timer-hero, timer-bar).

## 4. Not done / uncertain
- **Service change needed (not made; `vesper/` belongs to another agent):** `/api/history` ignores query parameters and returns only the last 24 h in 5-minute averages (`Store.history()`, `vesper/storage.py` line 73), although `sensor()` keeps 30 days of 30-second samples. Endpoint needed: `GET /api/history?hours=H&bucket=S` (H up to 720, S in seconds; the page sends `hours=168&bucket=3600` and `hours=720&bucket=10800`), same row shape `[{at, temperature, humidity}]`, averages per bucket, ascending `at`, about 2000 rows at most, `hours` and `bucket` validated and clamped. Until then the 7d and 30d views show the same 24 h with the note "ONLY THE LAST 24 H IS AVAILABLE". The standalone demo answers unknown paths with the state object; the page detects and ignores that.
- Node Scope: if `main.js` copied `link`, `fw` and `sensor` from `node_status` into `state.device`, a Node Scope opened later would show them at once instead of waiting up to a second. No real `node_status` was seen, only synthetic events carrying the documented fields.
- The lamp levels (comfort 25 % of full, chronometer bar about 30 %) are untested on the real lamps; the firmware applies gamma, so they may look dimmer than the screen mirror. `COMFORT_LEVEL` in `utilities.js` is the number to adjust.
- Once Chronometer (with a timer) or Atmosphere sets the lamps it keeps them until left (the app context has no release call), so the host's focus spot is not shown on those screens meanwhile.
- Today's low/high come from averaged samples; formula accuracy is that of the formulas (dew point about 0.4 C, heat index about 1.3 F).
